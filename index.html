Chicagoland Industrial Investment Sales

I specialize in the investment sales of industrial assets ranging from 10,000 to 100,000 square feet across Cook, DeKalb, DuPage, Grundy, Kane, Kendall, Lake, McHenry, and Will counties in Illinois.

The guides below are free resources I maintain for owners, buyers, and tenants in this market: the questions to ask, the records to check, and the official offices to check them with. Search the guides for a term, a county, or a topic.

Where to start

  • Thinking about selling? The selling guide covers who buys buildings in this size range, how they underwrite, what to assemble, and how a sale runs.
  • Buying for your business or as an investment? Pair the building checklist with the purchase-diligence guide; suitability and value are separate questions.
  • Leasing space? Compare total occupancy cost on the same basis, not advertised rent.
  • Researching a specific county? The nine-county hub links each county’s planning, zoning, parcel, and assessment offices.

Official starting points

  • City of Chicago zoning map and Department of Buildings — city zoning, permits, and inspections.
  • Cook County Assessor and Cook County Treasurer — assessment and tax-bill research serve different purposes.
  • Assessment offices for all nine counties — in the county hub.
  • Chicago Metropolitan Agency for Planning — regional planning context, not property-specific verification.
Scope. “Chicago real estate” here means commercial and industrial property, not homes. The nine counties are the ones I work in, not an official definition of Chicagoland; Indiana and Wisconsin are outside this site. These pages are educational—not listings, appraisals, or legal, tax, engineering, or investment advice.
