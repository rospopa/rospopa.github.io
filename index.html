Chicago commercial real estate resources

A practical starting point for researching business property, comparing industrial space, and preparing for a lease or purchase. These guides focus on the questions to ask and the public records to check—not on listings or market predictions.

Start with the decision, not the search term

  • Looking for space to lease? Write down your use, timing, size range, and operating requirements. Read the industrial guide if loading, production, or storage matters; then compare lease costs on the same basis.
  • Buying a building for your business? Combine the property-research and industrial checklists with the purchase-diligence guide. Occupancy suitability and investment value are separate questions.
  • Evaluating an investment? Begin with the actual leases, expenses, and capital plan. A building’s location alone does not establish income, liquidity, or a return.
Scope and limits. “Chicago real estate” here means commercial and industrial property, not residential home buying. Chicago city records do not cover every suburban municipality; Cook County sources do not cover the entire metropolitan region. These educational pages are not brokerage listings, appraisals, or legal, tax, engineering, or investment advice.

Useful official starting points

  • City of Chicago zoning map — an initial zoning lookup, followed by confirmation of the proposed use.
  • Chicago Department of Buildings — permitting, inspections, and code-related research.
  • Cook County Assessor and Cook County Treasurer — assessment and tax-bill research serve different purposes.
  • Chicago Metropolitan Agency for Planning — regional planning context, not a substitute for property-specific verification.
