Buying and investing

Is a 10,000 to 20,000 SF building a sensible first industrial investment?

Buying and investing · How each side may see it: simulated editorial perspectives

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

I own some residential rentals and want to move into industrial. Is a small single-tenant building too risky as a first deal?

Owner-user perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Companies like mine use small buildings. We found very few choices under 20,000 SF with a decent yard.

Commercial lender perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

We see many first industrial buyers at this size. We compare lease and loan terms and ask about liquidity if the tenant leaves.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

Yes, if you underwrite vacancy. A single tenant means 100% leased or 0%. Estimate downtime, improvements and commissions, and your ability to carry debt and taxes during re-leasing.

Functional infill buildings often have deep demand, including future owner-users. Read the single- versus multi-tenant discussion.

Two buildings are listed at the same cap rate. Why would one be the better buy?

Buying and investing · How each side may see it: simulated editorial perspectives

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Same size, same county, same cap rate on the offering memorandum. How do I tell them apart?

Property manager perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Compare actual tenant reimbursements: two "NNN" leases can have very different recoveries under their CAM and roof clauses.

Tenant operations perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Ask about tenant satisfaction. The rent roll will not show that we are about to outgrow the space.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

A cap rate captures one year. Compare in-place and market rent, lease term and options, expense recoveries, roof and pavement age, post-sale taxes, and functionality for the next tenant.

Below-market rent and a short lease can offer upside at the same cap rate if you can afford rollover. Use the cap-rate calculator with adjusted NOI, not the marketing figure.

Should an investor ever buy a vacant industrial building?

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Vacant buildings seem cheaper per square foot. What is the catch?

Commercial lender perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Without leases, financing means a lower lending value and more equity, or a higher rate bridge loan.

Owner-user perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

You compete with owner-users like me, who can pay more for a vacant building we plan to use.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

Sometimes. Price a lease-up for carry (taxes, insurance, utilities, debt), improvements, commissions, and free rent. It suits functional buildings in steady-demand locations when you can fund the wait.

Owner-users may outbid investors, often using SBA 504 financing. See owner-user vs. investor buyers.

Commercial lender perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Leasing and tenants

Leasing and tenants · How each side may see it: simulated editorial perspectives

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Flexibility costs landlords money. Ask for expansion and termination rights, but expect to pay for them.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

Focus on total cost and flexibility: pass-throughs (taxes, insurance, CAM, management, capital items); roof, HVAC, and dock repair or replacement; renewal and expansion; assignment and sublease; restoration; and outdoor storage and trailer rights.

Compare all-in cost over the term. See the lease cost section.

Can we sign a one-year lease on industrial space?

Leasing and tenants · How each side may see it: simulated editorial perspectives

We need space for a project that may last 12 to 18 months.

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Short leases barely repay landlords' commissions and improvements. Expect as-is space and higher rent.

Property manager perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Look for built-out space or a sublease nearing expiration.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

Sometimes, with trade-offs: as-is space, higher rent, fewer concessions, and possibly a larger deposit. Consider subleases with 12 to 24 months left, older multi-tenant buildings, or third-party logistics (3PL) warehousing.

If you may need more time, negotiate a renewal option now.

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

Ask a roofing consultant for an infrared or moisture survey and core samples. Dry insulation may support cheaper restoration or coating; wet insulation usually favors replacement.

Illinois taxes and incentives · How each side may see it: simulated editorial perspectives

Owner-user perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Our assessed value rose much more than we expected.

Tax consultant perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Watch Cook's short township appeal window; the Assessor publishes dates.

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Appeals are routine for income property. Most owners appeal regularly.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

How do I estimate taxes for a DuPage or Will County building I am buying?

Illinois taxes and incentives · How each side may see it: simulated editorial perspectives

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

I am used to Cook County. What changes in the collar counties?

Tax consultant perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

The township assessor is your first call. The supervisor of assessments and the Board of Review come next.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

Outside Cook, township assessments generally use one-third of market value with county equalization. Estimate post-purchase value, apply the assessment level and equalization factor, subtract exemptions and multiply by the tax-code rate.

Confirm with the township assessor. See Cook vs. collar county differences.

Financing

How does an SBA 504 loan work for buying our own warehouse?

Financing · How each side may see it: simulated editorial perspectives

Owner-user perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

We are a growing distributor and want to buy instead of lease.

Commercial lender perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

The occupancy rules matter. For an existing building, the business generally needs to occupy at least 51%.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

SBA 504 combines a bank first mortgage, an SBA-backed Certified Development Company second loan and a business down payment, often 10% but sometimes higher. The 504 portion offers long-term fixed rates.

The business must meet SBA eligibility rules and generally occupy at least 51% of an existing building. See the SBA’s 504 program page and contact a CDC early.

Recourse or non-recourse debt on a small industrial building?

Financing · How each side may see it: simulated editorial perspectives

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

My bank offers a better rate with a full personal guaranty.

Commercial lender perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Small bank loans are usually recourse; non-recourse is more common for larger loans, life companies and CMBS lenders.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

Compare the full package: local-bank recourse debt may offer flexibility and lower costs but risks personal assets. Non-recourse limits that risk but often adds “bad boy” carve-outs, stricter terms and yield-maintenance or defeasance penalties.

Some lenders offer partial recourse or burn-offs after stabilization.

How do prepayment penalties affect when I can sell?

Financing · How each side may see it: simulated editorial perspectives

Private investor perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

I want flexibility to sell in three to five years.

Commercial lender perspectiveSimulated viewpoint, not a real member or quote

Step-down penalties, such as 3-2-1, are common on bank loans. Fixed-rate CMBS and life company loans tend to use yield maintenance or defeasance.

Editorial note by Pavlo RospopaSite author guidance, not an accepted member answer

Large penalties can force a hold or buyer loan assumption. Match terms to your expected hold; confirm whether assumption is allowed.

Model penalties at different rates; yield maintenance costs more when rates fall.
