Commercial property questions: forums and industry voices

General education, not advice on a particular property, lease, loan or tax position.

By Pavlo Rospopa · Last updated October 10, 2026

How to read these discussions

Public forum comments, industry interviews and official assessment guidance offer different perspectives on income, leases, building fit and owner decisions. Pavlo Rospopa adds a brief editorial take and links to the relevant guide.

Short quotations link to their original sources. Speakers and publishers are not members, clients or endorsers of this site; their inclusion does not imply affiliation. Original spelling is preserved, and individual views are not verified market evidence.

Sources include BiggerPockets, Hacker News, SIOR, NAIOP's Development magazine, Northmarq and the Cook County Assessor's Office. Examples cover general commercial, retail, office and industrial property; not every source concerns Illinois. Dates describe the original post or publication, not today's market. Sources checked 2026-10-10.

Buying and valuation

Is a NNN property an easy first investment?

On the other hand, you have more tenant-side risk because it can take longer to find a replacement tenant than in something like a single family house.

Greg Scott on BiggerPockets · Sep 2022

Commercial landlords want long term tenants, not people going out of business in one or two years.

ww520 on Hacker News · Jul 2015 · Forum comment about startup tenants

Pavlo's take

Pavlo separates expense shifting from vacancy risk. For a single-tenant building, budget downtime, improvements, commissions and carrying costs before treating a net lease as low-maintenance income.

Read the guide: Single-tenant versus multi-tenant underwriting.

What does a cap rate leave out?

Risk vs reward will depend on the length of the lease, the tenant quality, and your ability to fill vacancies.

Greg Scott on BiggerPockets · Jan 2024

Pavlo's take

Pavlo compares documented NOI, lease expiry, recovery clauses and capital needs, not just the advertised rate. A cap rate is a one-year snapshot; a hold-period model tests rollover and sale assumptions.

Read the guide: DCF, lease rollover and exit-cap assumptions.

Should a purchase price use actual or projected NOI?

At the same time, you don't want to pay on the proforma because you'd be paying someone else now for something you would have to put in work to achieve further down the line.

Michael Le on BiggerPockets · Jul 2017

As a result, insurance has become one of the fastest growing operating expenses for property owners that directly impacts cash flow, debt service coverage and collateral values,

Whitney Ronning in Northmarq · Apr 2026 · Insurance interview

Pavlo's take

Pavlo starts with verified leases, collections and expenses, then models potential income separately. Achieving projected rent can require downtime, capital and leasing costs; a vacant building also has value beyond its current NOI.

Read the guide: Actual, forward and stabilized NOI.

Where should owners look for comparable cap rates?

Call the local brokers in that area. The current data on the internet is behind so you need to find out what sold recently.

John M. on BiggerPockets · Nov 2023

You can track asking rents but you can't really get a handle on actual rent paid since there are many deal factors (renovations, free rent, triple net etc.) that would change the numbers significantly.

larrys on Hacker News · Nov 2013 · Forum comment about transaction data

Pavlo's take

Pavlo tests comparability by location, function, tenant, lease term and the NOI convention. A reported sale or online average is a starting point, not proof of a rate for a particular Illinois building.

Read the guide: Cap rate, income and investment diligence.

Leases and CAM

How can CAM reconciliation be less confusing?

Then at year-end, you’re really just reconciling the final numbers instead of rebuilding twelve months of activity from bank statements and tenant emails.

Ashish Acharya on BiggerPockets · Sep 2026

Pavlo's take

Pavlo recommends keeping recoveries and expenses separate, with invoices, tenant shares, caps and exclusions documented throughout the year. Use the signed lease, not one formula for every tenant.

Read the guide: CAM reconciliation and cost controls.

Can management fees be recovered through CAM?

What is customary to be paid by tenants varies by what is negotiated.

Joel Owens on BiggerPockets · Nov 2019

Pavlo's take

Pavlo checks each lease’s definitions, exclusions, caps and allocation before adding a fee. An expense in the owner’s ledger is not automatically a permitted tenant charge; show any unrecovered share in NOI.

Read the guide: Lease expense allocation and recovery obligations.

Can an owner add late fees to a CAM bill?

It might be worth your time to pay for a lease review by a commercial retail attorney local to the asset.

Joel Owens on BiggerPockets · Feb 2022

Pavlo's take

Pavlo treats late-fee rights as a lease and legal-review question. Confirm payment deadlines, notice requirements and whether the clause covers reconciled charges before billing a penalty.

Read the guide: Lease review and occupancy costs.

Building fit and insurance

How should a small-bay owner choose suite sizes?

I would really recommend changing the approach from "if you build it they will come" to "who do I want to cater to and what do they need".

Joseph Gozlan on BiggerPockets · Dec 2022

The smaller space markets are very local in nature,

Mark Duclos in SIOR Pulse · Jun 2023 · Interview focused on Connecticut and western Massachusetts

In square footage, the pipeline is concentrated in megabuildings, which have seen the most decline in demand,

Lisa DeNight in NAIOP Development magazine · Winter 2023/24 · Historical national industrial outlook, not a small-bay forecast

Pavlo's take

Pavlo starts with the intended use, access, loading, power and permitted occupancy. Tenant interviews and local leasing evidence are more useful than a universal ideal bay size; test the proposed layout and approvals before building.

Read the guide: Industrial building types and specifications.

Why do contractor-yard rents differ?

Maybe look into why they vary so much, maybe some are offering more amenities, better security, better access, nicer property.

Grant Rothenburger on BiggerPockets · Aug 2018

Pavlo's take

Pavlo verifies outdoor-storage permission, lease rights, surface, drainage, security and actual truck paths. Two yards with similar area may support very different operations; a quoted rent does not establish usable access.

Read the guide: Truck access, swept paths and offtracking.

How should an owner compare insurance quotes?

The best policy (considering price and coverage) will vary by location, construction, loss history, use of property, etc.

John Mocker on BiggerPockets · Dec 2020

Policy exclusions or limitations can create compliance issues and unexpected gaps at renewal or claim time,

Matt Dvorak in Northmarq · Apr 2026 · Insurance interview

Lenders are placing greater emphasis on obtaining complete copies of insurance policies rather than relying on ACORD certificates, which are now seen as less dependable documentation,

Whitney Ronning in Northmarq · Apr 2026 · Lender-compliance perspective

Pavlo's take

Pavlo recommends comparable limits, valuation, exclusions and deductibles, reviewed with a licensed commercial insurance adviser. Update occupancy and building information before renewal; a cheaper quote may remove protection rather than reduce the same risk.

Read the guide: Commercial insurance coverage and renewal review.

Cook County taxes

Who can help review a Cook County tax appeal?

You are most definitely looking for a property tax attorney

Aaron Zimmerman on BiggerPockets · Nov 2025

Commercial property assessments are calculated by determining a property’s use, estimated income, market-level vacancy, collections loss, and expenses.

Cook County Assessor's Office · Jun 2026 · Official Palos Township assessment explanation

Pavlo's take

Pavlo starts with parcel records, assessment evidence and the correct appeal calendar. Confirm standing, representation rules and further appeal options with the relevant office and counsel; no adviser or appeal guarantees a lower bill.

Read the guide: Illinois owner tax planning and appeal routes.

Refinancing

How should an owner plan for a balloon payment?

Of course tides and environments can change drastically between " re financing" which is causing a lot of concern now, especially for retail

Will G. on BiggerPockets · Oct 2017

From an equity investor’s perspective, you’re willing to take a risk if you think that there is a potential big payout. If you’re a lender, there is no upside besides getting paid back.

Jamie Woodwell in NAIOP Development magazine · Winter 2023/24 · Historical discussion of office lending and credit risk

Pavlo's take

Pavlo models refinancing as a risk, not a guaranteed exit. Check maturity, prepayment terms, lease expiry, lender-adjusted NOI, DSCR and cash needed if the new loan is smaller than the balance due.

Read the guide: Refinancing, DSCR, LTV and debt yield.

Renewing versus re-leasing

Should an owner hold out for a higher renewal rent?

The landlord doesn't want you to to leave but only to the extent that finding a new tenant costs more than the discount against the current market price they'd have to give you to stay.

AnthonyMouse on Hacker News · Jun 2026 · Forum comment about commercial vacancy

And because the pond is more shallow, landlords, while still in the drivers’ seat, cannot push rates up like they used to.

Mark Duclos in SIOR Pulse · Jun 2023 · Historical Hartford warehouse leasing discussion

Pavlo's take

Pavlo compares the renewal with a documented re-leasing scenario: downtime, carrying costs, tenant improvements, commissions and tenant credit. Neither a forum view nor an older regional interview establishes today's achievable rent; use current local evidence and test the cash-flow trade-off.

Read the guide: Lease rollover and downtime in DCF; try the free cash-flow calculator.

Selling and 1031 timing

How can sale and replacement-property timing be coordinated?

The attorney negotiating the sale and purchase should hedge the risk snd build in flexibility on both ends.

Joshua Gorsky on BiggerPockets · Aug 2024

Pavlo's take

Pavlo recommends coordinating counsel, financing and a qualified intermediary before the sale closes. A reverse exchange requires specialist planning; sale-contract flexibility does not extend the statutory identification or completion deadlines.

Read the guide: 1031 exchanges and retirement exit planning.
